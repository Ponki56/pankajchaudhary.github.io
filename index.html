01 / About

About me

I work on understanding the behaviour of fluids and rocks in the subsurface, particularly where injection-driven pressure and temperature changes interact with reservoir and caprock integrity.

Research direction

Coupled hydro-mechanical and thermo-hydro-mechanical modelling for geological CO₂ storage and subsurface energy applications.

Research objective

Improve understanding of subsurface processes that influence storage performance, injectivity, and geomechanical integrity.

02 / Research

Research interests
